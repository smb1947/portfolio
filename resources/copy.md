# intro copy
The things I pursue tend to follow a simple rhythm: WHAT, WHY, HOW, COMMIT, MONITOR, and ITERATE.

I start by discovering WHAT I want to take on next. When an idea keeps returning, I ask WHY I can’t let it go. Once the reason feels strong enough, I work out HOW to make it real and COMMIT to the work. I MONITOR my progress and ITERATE on my approach until what began as a hope becomes real.

I rarely know exactly how I’ll get there when I begin. I learn what the challenge asks of me and become more capable and resilient along the way. The work changes me, and who I become shapes the next WHAT I pursue. That cycle has taken me from an engineering college in India to Microsoft Azure, the Foster MBA, and AWS—and it continues to shape what I chase next.
