# intro copy
I’m an AI-first technical product builder who turns messy customer problems into clear direction and products people adopt and trust. The way I build products and myself follows a personal rhythm: **WHAT, WHY, HOW, COMMIT, DISCIPLINE, MONITOR, and ITERATE**.

I start by discovering **WHAT** I want to take on next. When an idea keeps returning, I ask **WHY** I can’t let it go. Once the reason feels strong enough, I seek out different perspectives, work out **HOW** to make it real, and **COMMIT** to the work. I rarely know exactly how I’ll get there when I begin. My **WHY** gives me a reason to keep going, and **DISCIPLINE** carries me through the distance between hope and achievement. I **MONITOR** my progress and **ITERATE** on my approach until what I imagined becomes real.

Through that journey, I build the skills, resilience, and perspective the challenge demands. What I learn and who I become define my **NEXT WHAT**.

This way of living has taken me from an engineering college in India to Microsoft Azure, the Foster MBA, and AWS.
